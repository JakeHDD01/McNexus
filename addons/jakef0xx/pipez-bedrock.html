Pipez

Pipez adds a transportation system to Minecraft, allowing players to transfer items, fluids, and energy between machines, generators, and storage blocks.

It includes Item Pipes, Fluid Pipes, Energy Pipes, Universal Pipes, upgrades, and a Pipe Wrench for configuring connections and extraction.

Addon Compatibility

Other addon developers can integrate their machines using Pipez compatibility tags and Scoreboards.

Tags:

- "pipez:item_storage" — Item storage.
- "pipez:fluid_storage" — Fluid storage.
- "pipez:energy_storage" — Energy storage.
- "pipez:in" — Receive energy.
- "pipez:out" — Provide energy.
- "pipez:both" — Receive and provide energy.

Energy Scoreboards:

- "energy" — Stored energy.
- "energyCap" — Maximum capacity.
- "energyExp" — Energy exponent.
- "energyCapExp" — Capacity exponent.
- "be" and "beCap" — Alternative energy storage format.

These systems allow compatible addons to send and receive resources through Pipez networks.
