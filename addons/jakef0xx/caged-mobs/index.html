Caged Mobs
Caged Mobs adds new items and blocks to Minecraft, including mob cages, automatic collection cages, a crystallized experience block, and a Nether Star-infused Netherite block.

It also introduces DNA collectors made from different materials, along with upgrades for speed, looting, experience, cooking, and more. New materials such as sponge fragments, dragon scales, and Nether Star fragments are also included.
