Endless Avaritia

Endless Avaritia brings the ultimate crafting experience to Minecraft Bedrock, introducing powerful materials, machines, tools, weapons, and Infinity armor.

The addon features Neutronium Collectors, Neutron Compressors, Singularity crafting, and extremely powerful Infinity equipment, recreating the challenging progression of the original Java mod.

It also includes an integrated JEI-style recipe viewer, allowing players to explore crafting recipes and discover how to create different items and materials.

Addon Compatibility

Developers can make their own blocks compatible with Endless Avaritia's Singularity system by adding the following component to their block:

""tag:endlessjake:singularity_color_gold": {}"

Replace "gold" with one of the supported color identifiers:

"gold", "orange", "yellow", "red", "brown", "lime", "green", "teal", "aqua", "cyan", "lightblue", "blue", "purple", "magenta", "pink", "white", "gray", "dark", "black".

These colors are approximations and may differ from the original Java mod, allowing developers to create generic custom singularities for their own addons.
